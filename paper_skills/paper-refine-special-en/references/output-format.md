# Output Format

Choose the output based on the scope of the input.

## Single Paragraph Input

Use:

- Part 1 [Polish Plan]
- Part 2 [Final LaTeX]
- Part 3 [Translation]
- Part 4 [Revision Log]

`Polish Plan` should briefly state:

- the core logic issue if any
- the compression approach
- the intended sentence-level polishing focus

## Multi-Paragraph Subsection, Section, Or Full Paper

Use:

- Part 1 [Section Outline] or [Document Outline]
- Part 2 [Final LaTeX]
- Part 3 [Translation]
- Part 4 [Revision Log]

## Revision Log Requirements

Always summarize changes under these headings:

- Logic restructuring
- Compression
- Sentence-level polishing
- Reviewer-pass fixes

Do not expose raw internal drafts unless the user explicitly asks for them.
