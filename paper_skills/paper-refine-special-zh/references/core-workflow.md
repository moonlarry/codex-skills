# Core Workflow

Follow this pipeline exactly. Do not skip steps.

## Phase 1: Global Logic Check

Check the text at the correct scope:

- Single paragraph: examine sentence order, causal links,指代是否清楚，以及结论是否有支撑
- Multi-paragraph subsection: examine paragraph roles, transitions, duplication, and whether the subsection advances one coherent purpose
- Full section or full paper: examine section order, repeated claims, terminology drift, contribution-to-evidence alignment, and whether the global narrative closes properly

Internal questions:

- 当前结构是否合理
- 是否存在功能重复的段落或小节
- 是否有结论先于证据出现
- 是否有段间或节间承接缺失
- 是否有用词或术语在不同位置漂移
- 是否有实验结论超出了证据支持范围

## Phase 2: Outline Reconstruction

If the input is longer than one paragraph, reconstruct the outline first.

Assign a role to each paragraph or subsection:

- 背景
- 结论
- 证据
- 解释
- 过渡
- 局限性

Then create a compact section or document outline and rewrite from that outline instead of editing the original order in place.

## Phase 3: Compression

Compress after the outline is stable.

Compression targets:

- 重复表述
- 空泛铺垫
- 低信息量过渡句
- 可合并的相近句
- 不必要的修饰和限定

Compression level:

- 默认中高强度
- 保留关键术语、技术事实、实验参数、对比对象、结论边界、公式与引用
- 不得损坏论证主线和证据链

## Phase 4: Sentence-Level Polishing

Polish sentence by sentence on top of the compressed draft.

Requirements:

- 提升学术表达的准确性、自然度和凝练度
- 修复病句和衔接问题
- 保持术语一致
- 不脱离压缩稿进行整体另写
- 中文保持现代学术书面语，不引入公文腔

## Phase 5: Reviewer-Style Final Pass

Read the final draft like a skeptical reviewer.

Check for:

- 结论是否夸大
- 证据与结论是否闭环
- 段间或节间是否仍有跳跃
- 是否仍存在容易被审稿人质疑的含混表述

If needed, make minimal final revisions before presenting the answer.
