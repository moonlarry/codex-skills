# Reviewer Independence

Reviewer independence prevents the paper executor's assumptions from becoming the audit result.

## Required Separation

- The paper executor collects inputs and applies fixes.
- The paper architect/reviewer diagnoses the artifact from the supplied evidence.
- The reviewer should receive source artifacts, raw evidence, and explicit questions, not the executor's preferred conclusion.

## Fresh Context Cases

Use a fresh reviewer context for:

- proof audits
- experiment claim audits
- citation audits
- experiment-result-to-claim judgments
- rebuttal stress tests

## What Not To Pass

Do not pass prior audit summaries, improvement logs, or executor-written interpretations unless the skill explicitly asks for them. When reviewing a fix, pass the revised section and the acceptance criteria.

## Trace

Record enough trace material for a later reader to see what was reviewed, what the reviewer concluded, and what the executor changed afterward.
